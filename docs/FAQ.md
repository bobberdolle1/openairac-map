# ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

### Q1: Is OpenAIRAC Map completely free?
**Yes.** OpenAIRAC Map is free open-source software licensed under the GNU General Public License (GPLv3). There are no paid tiers, subscriptions, paywalls, or account registrations required.

---

### Q2: Do I need a Navigraph subscription to use OpenAIRAC Map?
**No.** OpenAIRAC includes a comprehensive worldwide public aeronautical baseline (FAA CIFP, OurAirports, OpenFlightmaps, France SIA) active out-of-the-box. You can plan routes, calculate airways, view approach plates, and fly without any commercial subscription.

---

### Q3: Why are some airports or procedures in certain countries missing or marked `SOURCE_REQUIRED`?
OpenAIRAC strictly respects international copyright and state aviation distribution policies. Certain national authorities (such as Russian CAICA) do not grant permission for third-party public redistribution. Users who legally possess official AIP packages for these countries can import them locally via the **Local AIP Vault** for private simulation use.

---

### Q4: Can I use OpenAIRAC for real-world flying?
**ABSOLUTELY NOT.** OpenAIRAC is strictly for **FLIGHT SIMULATION ONLY**. It is not certified by the FAA, EASA, or any aviation authority, and must never be used for real flight planning or navigation.

---

### Q5: Does OpenAIRAC Map support Microsoft Flight Simulator (MSFS 2024 and 2020)?
**Yes.** OpenAIRAC Map connects natively to MSFS 2024 and 2020 via SimConnect, showing real-time aircraft telemetry, AI traffic, and flight plan synchronization.

---

### Q6: Does OpenAIRAC Map support X-Plane 12 and 11?
**Yes.** OpenAIRAC Map connects to X-Plane 12 and 11 via the included `Little Xpconnect` plugin, providing moving map tracking, weather, and FMS flight plan export.

---

### Q7: How do I update OpenAIRAC Map when a new version is released?
Go to **OpenAIRAC → Check for OpenAIRAC Updates...** in the menu. When an update is available, download the latest installer (`OpenAIRAC-Map-<version>-Setup.exe`) and run it. The installer will automatically upgrade the program while **preserving all your custom flight plans, settings, and Local AIP Vault data**.

---

### Q8: What is the relationship between OpenAIRAC Map and Little Navmap?
OpenAIRAC Map is proudly derived from **Little Navmap** by **Alexander Barthel**. It integrates the OpenAIRAC temporal aeronautical engine, official government charts, NOAA weather, and the AI Crew Gateway into the proven Little Navmap moving map foundation.
