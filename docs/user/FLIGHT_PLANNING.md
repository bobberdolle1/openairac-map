# Flight Planning & Active Flight Guide

## 1. Creating a Flight Plan

1. Right-click your departure airport (e.g. `KJFK`) and choose **Set as Departure**.
2. Right-click your destination airport (e.g. `LFPG`) and choose **Set as Destination**.
3. Select an active runway, SID departure procedure, and STAR arrival procedure.
4. Add enroute waypoints or airways along the route corridor.

---

## 2. Active Flight Dashboard

When connected to your simulator:
* **Flight Phase**: Displays current phase (`Preflight`, `TaxiOut`, `Departure`, `Cruise`, `Arrival`, `Approach`, `Final`, `Landing`).
* **Next Waypoint & ETA**: Computes real-time distance and estimated arrival time based on actual groundspeed.
* **Cross-Track Distance (XTK)**: Shows deviation in nautical miles left or right of course.
* **Planning TOD**: Automatically calculates Top-of-Descent point based on current altitude and destination elevation.
