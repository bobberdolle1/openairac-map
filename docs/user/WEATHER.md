# Live Aviation Weather & Preflight Briefing

## 1. Surface Observations & Forecasts

OpenAIRAC Map queries NOAA AviationWeather.gov in real-time:
* **METAR**: Decoded surface wind, temperature, dewpoint, visibility, altimeter (QNH), and flight category (`VFR`, `MVFR`, `IFR`, `LIFR`).
* **TAF**: Terminal Aerodrome Forecast with automatic evaluation of conditions at your estimated time of arrival (ETA).
* **SIGMET Polygons**: Severe weather hazard areas displayed on the moving map.

---

## 2. Integrated Flight Briefing

Go to **Tools > Flight Briefing...** to generate a preflight briefing containing departure/destination weather, active enroute SIGMETs, chart links, and active VATSIM ATC coverage.
