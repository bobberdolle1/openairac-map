# Aeronautical Charts & PDF Viewing Guide

## 1. Accessing Charts

* Open **Window > Charts Dock** or press `F6`.
* Enter any airport ICAO (e.g. `KJFK`, `LFPG`) in the search bar.
* Filter by category: **Airport Diagram**, **Departure (SID)**, **Arrival (STAR)**, **Approach (IAP)**, or **General**.
* Click any plate to download and view in the embedded PDF viewer.

---

## 2. Contextual Chart Automation

The **Chart Context Engine** suggests relevant charts as your flight progresses:
* **Taxi-out**: Departure Airport Diagram
* **Takeoff / Climb**: Active SID plate
* **Descent / Arrival**: Active STAR plate
* **Approach**: Active Instrument Approach (IAP) plate
* **Landing / Taxi-in**: Destination Airport Diagram & Parking chart

Choose your preferred automation mode under **Settings > Chart Automation**: `Off`, `Suggest` (default), or `AutoOpen`.
